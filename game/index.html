Controls (tap anywhere to close)

Interface:

Tap select a unit or building

Tap with units selected: send them. Tap a resource to gather, an enemy or creep to attack, your unfinished building to build, a farm to farm.

Tap the ground with a Town Hall or Barracks selected: rally point

Double-tap a unit: every one of that type on screen

Hold, then drag draw a selection box (or turn on Box)

Hold a unit add it to or remove it from the selection · hold empty ground deselect

Drag move the map · pinch zoom

Rail Build (picks the nearest peasant if none is selected), Box, Idle peasant, Army, Screen (your units on screen), Move/Attack (what a tap on the ground does), Clear, Map

Building the ghost is pinned mid-screen: tap to move it, tap it again or Place to build, Repeat keeps placing. Palisades: drag a line.

Slots 1-5 tap an empty slot to save the selection, tap a full one to select it, twice to jump there, hold to overwrite

Minimap tap or drag to look · hold to send the selection there

Hold a command button see what it does

Left-click / drag select · Shift add/remove

Double-click or Ctrl-click all of that type on screen

Right-click move, gather, build, attack, drop off, garrison, pick up artifact

Shift + right-click queue orders (waypoints, several builds)

Right-click with a building selected: rally point (on a resource: new peasants gather it)

Ctrl+1-9 make a group · Shift+1-9 add · 1-9 select · twice to jump

. idle peasant · , idle military · H Town Hall

Space jump to the last alert · Del delete

Q W E R T / A S D F G / Z X C V B the command grid

Peasant: Q economic buildings, W military buildings, then the building's key; Shift keeps placing; drag for palisades

Town Hall: Q Peasant (Shift = 5) · Z Town Bell · X All Clear · C ungarrison

Military: Q W E R stances · A attack-move · S stop · D fetch artifact · F patrol · G garrison · Z X C V formations · B delete

Arrows, screen edge or middle-drag scroll · wheel zoom

Minimap: click to look · right-click to send units

F3 pause · + / - speed · F2 technology tree · F10 menu · Esc cancel

Higher ground: +25% damage; lower: -25% (as in AoE2).

Creep camps ward the crystal and iron beside them. Clear the camp to mine it and claim its artifacts.
